Descriptions

One line

AI Conf Asia 2027 brings Asia’s AI community together in Kathmandu, Nepal, on January 8–10, 2027.

Short · about 50 words

AI Conf Asia 2027 is a three-day gathering for the people building with AI, held in Kathmandu, Nepal, on January 8–10, 2027. Practitioners from across Asia and beyond share how they build, ship and scale AI, followed by a community hike in the hills above Kathmandu.

Long · about 150 words

AI Conf Asia 2027 brings together engineers, designers, founders and product builders shaping the future of AI. It takes place in Kathmandu, Nepal, from January 8 to 10, 2027. The 2026 edition drew 450+ attendees from 100+ Nepali companies and sold out ten days before the event. In 2027 the conference grows into a more international meeting of AI builders, with local and international speakers sharing practical lessons from real projects, from research to production. The three days begin with a community dinner for speakers, sponsors and organizers on January 8. The main day of talks and keynotes is on January 9. On January 10 everyone heads outdoors for a community hike with Himalayan views, a wwktm tradition since 2018. AI Conf Asia is organized by Web Weekend Kathmandu (wwktm), the community behind Tech Kura Kani meetups and BarCamp Kathmandu.
