What is AI Conf Asia 2027?
A three-day regional AI conference in Kathmandu, Nepal. It brings together engineers, researchers, designers and founders from across Asia for a community dinner, a day of talks and keynotes, and the wwktm community hike.
When and Where is it Happening?
January 8–10, 2027 in Kathmandu, Nepal. Jan 8 is a dinner for speakers, sponsors, organizers and Micro Sponsor ticket holders. Jan 9 is the main conference, and Jan 10 is the limited-capacity community hike.
Who Should Attend?
Engineers, researchers, designers, founders and product folks who build with AI or build for AI. If you’re curious about applied AI and want to meet the people doing the work, you belong here.
How Much Are Tickets?
Believer tickets are NPR 4,000, Regular tickets are NPR 6,000, and Micro Sponsor tickets are NPR 15,000. International prices are USD 55, USD 75 and USD 165. See ticket details and availability.
Will Recordings be Available?
Selected talks and the event recap go up on YouTube after the conference. The AI Conf Asia 2026 recap is already live if you want a feel for the room.
